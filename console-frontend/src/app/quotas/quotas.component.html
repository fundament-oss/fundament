Quotas

What your organization may build: how many clusters, and how many nodes of each machine type in each region. A node pool counts with its maximum size, whether or not the autoscaler ever reaches it, across all your clusters. A deleted cluster and its pools count until the platform has torn them down.

Quotas are set by the platform operator and only stop something new: a cluster, a node pool, or a pool growing. To request an increase in quotas, contact a Fundament operator.
